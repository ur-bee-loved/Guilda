Recently reviewed

Postman tells us that the medium of a message is not neutral, because the medium shapes and mutates the message according to its agenda. A medium subtly defines the set of possible messages, and has its own ideas of “good” and “bad” messages. More concretely, you wouldn’t write law as poetry or play a song by painting a picture. That’d be dumb! Postman tells us that’s precisely what we are doing by using television as the main medium of our cultural discourse.
